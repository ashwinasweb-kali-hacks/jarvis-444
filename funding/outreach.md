# 72-hour funding run (5 → 8 Nov)

The honest constraint: **no government scheme in India pays out in 3 days.** NIDHI-PRAYAS,
TIDE 2.0 and TANSEED all run an application → screening → presentation → committee → disbursement
chain. Realistic turnaround is 6 to 12 weeks. Anyone promising a cheque by 8 November is
either misunderstanding the scheme or lying to you.

So the 72-hour plan splits into two tracks that run in parallel.

---

## Track A — money that can actually land by 8 Nov

| Source | Realistic amount | Time to cash | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| UPI contributions page (`funding/collect.html`) | ₹2,000 – ₹25,000 | minutes to 2 days | Share on WhatsApp/Instagram with a concrete ask, not "please support" |
| Hardware instead of cash | full line items | 1–3 days | Ask for a spare 8 GB Android phone, a used Jetson, a 4G dongle |
| Bank personal loan (₹1–2 L) | ₹1,00,000+ | 24–72 h | Pre-approved offers in your banking app; instant disbursal to account |
| Advance on freelance/contract work | ₹10,000 – ₹50,000 | 1–2 days | Invoice at 50% upfront |
| Family / friend loan with written terms | varies | same day | Write it down, even one line |

**None of these are grants.** They are how the hardware actually gets bought this week.

## Track B — the grant track (money in December, not November)

Submit the application *and* run Track A. The grant then repays the hardware or funds round two.

1. `nidhi-prayas.org/innovators/` → read the grant guidelines
2. `nidhi-prayas.org` → PRAYAS Centres → pick the one nearest you, note its contact
3. Email that centre's manager with `JARVIS_OS_OnePager.pdf` attached, ask for the **next innovator call date**
4. Same day: `msh.meity.gov.in/schemes/tide` → find a TIDE 2.0 incubator taking applications
5. Same day: `startuptn.in` → register interest for TANSEED 9.0 (opening ≈ Dec 2026)

## What not to do

- Do not tell anyone the money is guaranteed.
- Do not accept money described as investment, equity or profit-sharing on a UPI page —
  that is an unregulated securities offering.
- Do not buy hardware on a promise of grant money. Buy after the money is in the account.
